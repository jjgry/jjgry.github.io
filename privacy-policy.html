Privacy policy

Calne Running Club uses personal information to administer membership, communicate club news and organise training and races.

Information you provide

If you contact the club, your name, email address and message are used to respond to your enquiry. The contact form on this static site opens an email draft in your email application; the message is sent only when you choose to send it.

When joining, the details you provide, including your name, date of birth, address and telephone number, are used for club administration and registration with England Athletics. Membership information is shared with England Athletics for that purpose and is not passed to other third parties.

Website data

This static version of the website does not provide public comments or user accounts and does not set login cookies. The original WordPress site may use cookies for comments and account functions. Embedded content from other websites can collect data and use cookies as if you had visited those websites directly.

If you upload photographs to a website, avoid files that contain embedded GPS location data (EXIF).

Retention and your rights

Club membership details are used only for club matters such as membership, training and races. You may ask to access, correct or delete information the club holds about you. Legal, safeguarding and accounting obligations may require some records to be kept.

To request deletion or correction, speak to Simon or Denise at a club session, or contact the club secretary.
