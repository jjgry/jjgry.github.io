Club Championship

The CRC Club Championship is a closed club championship that is designed to reward club representation within the club championship events along with strong individual performances.

The Championship for 2026 will comprise of 14 events. Wiltshire Road Race League (WRRL) fixtures are included to encourage CRC members to support the club and earn points in the league.

How the championship works

The men's and women's championships are scored separately. The member with the greatest number of points at the end of the championship wins.

At each championship race, the first CRC runner in each group receives 20 points, the second receives 19, the third 18, and so on. At parkruns, points are based on age grading.

You can claim an additional 20 points for each of the following events:

  • A half marathon (max 1, 20 points)
  • A 20 mile event (max 1, 20 points)
  • A marathon (max 1, 20 points)
  • An ultramarathon (max 2, 20 points each)

Contact Michelle Blower, the Club Championship Lead, when you complete an event that qualifies for bonus points.
