Social media policy

This policy applies to Calne Running Club members and people associated with the club, including athletes, families, coaches and volunteers. It supports safe, respectful and responsible online communication.

Think before you post

Social media is public, even when you expect a post to reach only a selected audience. Posts can be copied, forwarded and remain online indefinitely. You are responsible for what you publish, including material shared from others.

  • Could another person interpret your post differently from what you intend?
  • Could it affect someone's safety, security or privacy?
  • Is social media the right way to resolve the issue, or would a conversation or email be more suitable?
  • Could you be reacting in the heat of the moment?
  • Would you be comfortable with children, older people or the public reading it?

Representing the club

If authorised to represent Calne Running Club online, share only information you have permission to publish. Check that it is accurate and consistent with club values and policies. Club social account information and passwords remain the club's property.

Misuse

Online bullying and harassment are as serious as offline behaviour. Inappropriate use includes threats, abusive or offensive language, discriminatory or derogatory comments, sharing private information, spreading rumours, defamatory statements, or material that brings the club into disrepute. Do not use social media to air club grievances or encourage others to gang up on someone.

Messages to members through the club Facebook group should be positive or used to make practical arrangements.

Raising a concern

Report inappropriate club-related posts to a committee member, or to the Welfare Officer if a young person's welfare may be affected. The committee should review evidence and speak with the person involved, considering severity, context, whether the post was removed and whether an apology was made. Outcomes may include a verbal or written warning, temporary suspension, removal of social media access or, in serious or repeated cases, termination of membership.

Protect yourself online

  • Learn each platform's rules and review your privacy settings regularly.
  • Use strong passwords and up-to-date security software.
  • Keep personal details such as date of birth, location and contact information private.
  • Be cautious with links, unexpected offers and messages asking for information.
  • Consider carefully before sharing information or photographs of other people.

Use the same respect online that you would face to face. If you would not do it offline, do not do it online.
