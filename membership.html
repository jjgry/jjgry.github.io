Membership

Membership costs £40 per year, covering 1 April to 31 March. Fees are the same whenever you join, as England Athletics affiliation is paid for the full year. New England Athletics members joining between January and March are covered through 31 March of the following year.

Your membership includes England Athletics affiliation, coached Thursday training, led Tuesday social runs, club and league racing, the Club Championship, and a welcoming community with regular social events.

Come along for a week or two and try our sessions. New runners and experienced athletes are equally welcome.

How to Join

Email our secretary at secretary@calnerunningclub.co.uk with your name, date of birth, address and telephone number.

By providing your details, you understand they will be shared with England Athletics for registration. Members agree to follow the club policies and the UK Athletics Anti-Doping Rules.
