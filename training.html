Training

We meet twice a week on Tuesday and Thursday evenings.

Outside club nights, members get together for speed sessions and longer weekend social runs. These are shared in our members-only Facebook group. There is something for everybody, and nobody gets left behind.

Tuesday: Social Runs

Enjoy the Wiltshire countryside with us, starting from a different location each week, with groups organised by ability. Each group has a qualified leader and a tail runner. All abilities are catered for, and nobody is left behind.

Meet at 6:50pm for a 7pm start

Thursday: Structured Training

UK Athletics-qualified coaches Denise and Simon Nott lead sessions for beginners through to experienced runners. Training includes intervals, speed and tempo work, and sometimes strength and conditioning. Take each session at your own pace. One-to-one coaching is also available on request.

Meet at Beversbrook Sports Facility 6:50pm for a 7pm start

Run safely

  • Wear suitable clothing and footwear, including high-visibility kit and lights in the dark.
  • Listen to your run leader and loop back to the slowest runner as advised.
  • Take responsibility for your own safety and do not run when injured.
  • Please do not use portable music players during club runs.
