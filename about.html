About

Formed in 1992 by Mike Tawn and Dave Balkwill, the club serves Calne and the surrounding area. Today, around 120 members of all ages and abilities run with us.

We promote running as an enjoyable leisure activity, help members improve fitness and reach personal goals, and provide opportunities to compete in club and county championships. We strive to foster community spirit and to make running accessible to all.

Club Clothing

Wear Calne purple with pride at parkrun, races and training. Our signature race vests, T-shirts and long-sleeved tops are custom designed by the club and supplied by Ronhill. Stock is usually available in men's S, M, L and XL and women's sizes 8 to 16. Contact Denise or Simon to check availability.

  • Race vest: £21
  • T-shirt: £23
  • Long-sleeved top: £25

Hoodies, hats, snoods, soft-shell jackets and trousers featuring the club logo can be ordered from Sporting Billy.

London Marathon Club Place

The club receives at least one place in the TCS London Marathon, drawn by ballot at the Christmas dinner. To enter the club ballot, you must have been a club member for at least one year and provide evidence that you were unsuccessful in the public ballot.

Send your ballot rejection email to the club secretary to register your interest.
