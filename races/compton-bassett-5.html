Compton Bassett 5 Mile

Sunday 6 September 2026 · Wiltshire Road Race League

A fast, flat five-mile race on quiet country roads. The route starts about 250 metres beyond Compton Bassett Benson Hall (SN11 8RQ), follows the C15 and the Highway Loop, then returns to the hall. The course starts and finishes at 104m elevation, with a low point of 86m and a maximum gradient of 4.1%.

Race-day schedule

  • 8:00am: registration and on-the-day entries
  • 9:45am: race briefing outside Benson Hall
  • 9:55am: walk to the start
  • 10:00am: race start

Free parking is available nearby; follow marshal directions. Village hall registration and toilets are available. Chip timing is by DB Max, and finishers receive a locally made medal.

Entry and eligibility

Advance entry: £20 affiliated, £22 non-affiliated.

On-the-day entry, if places remain: £25 affiliated, £27 non-affiliated.

The field is limited to 300 runners. Minimum age is 16 on race day; under-18s need parent or guardian consent.
