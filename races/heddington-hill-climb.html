Heddington Hill Climb

Club members only — this is a closed club event.

Club members will have a chance to take on the gruelling challenge of racing for just over 1 mile — all uphill! It's an event that the club has held for decades, making it a real classic run for the club.

With an average incline of 7% and 200m total elevation, the video below shows what to expect (started from halfway up!)
