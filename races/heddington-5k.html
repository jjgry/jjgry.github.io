Heddington 5K Series

The UKA-licensed Heddington 5K is a fast, flat road race around the quiet country lanes of Heddington and a Wiltshire Road Race League fixture.

Race-day details

Race headquarters is Charles Willis Hall, Church Road, Heddington, SN11 0PH, next to the primary school. Toilets are available. Free parking is in the field opposite the Ivy Inn; follow the signs and marshals.

  • Entry: £12 affiliated, £14 non-affiliated
  • 6:00pm: registration
  • 7:15pm: briefing and walk to the start
  • 7:30pm: race start

Please be considerate to marshals, pedestrians and other road users. Follow the Highway Code and ARC rules. Portable music players are not permitted.
