Heddington 5K Series

The UKA-licensed Heddington 5K is a fast, flat road race around the quiet country lanes of Heddington. The 2026 event features as part of the Wiltshire Road Race League.

Race dates

Dates for 2027 races will be coming soon

Race-day details

Race headquarters is Charles Willis Hall, Church Road, Heddington, SN11 0PH, next to the primary school. Toilets are available. Free parking is in the field opposite the Ivy Inn; follow the signs and marshals.

The Route

A very fast and flat course around the quiet, picturesque country lanes of Heddington, exactly what you need for chasing down that PB!

Code of Conduct

Please be polite to all marshals on course, all pedestrians and other road users. Should you withdraw from the event for any reason, please alert one of the event staff and return your racing bib to them. Follow the Highway Code at all times and ARC rules.
